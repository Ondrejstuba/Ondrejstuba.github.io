Mission Five
The Last Stand

The year is 3032 AD

The eve of destruction is here...


The battle was brutal, and your forces were no match for the aliens.

They have now taken over the planet, and what remains of humanity is scattered and broken.

You reflect on all that has been lost and wonder if there was anything more you could have done.

But you know that you did everything in your power, and that your fight was not in vain.

You will continue to fight, for the sake of those who remain and for the hope that one day, Earth may be restored.

No other options remain, but to make one more - one last - stand.

In the name of humanity...
