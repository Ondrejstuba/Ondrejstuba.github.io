Mission Three

The year is 3027 AD

Many more skirmishes later...


In a last-ditch effort to turn the tide of the war, you and a small group of soldiers launch an attack on the alien mothership.

The journey is perilous, and you face countless obstacles and dangers.

But finally, you reach the heart of the enemy vessel, and a fierce battle ensues.

You fight with everything you've got, but it seems like the aliens have an endless supply of soldiers.

Your courageous effort might very well be your last.

You and your men will be lucky to escape, it is impossible to even consider an attack now...
