Objectives

As Captain Nick Theodore Vrenna your objective is to save the human race and defeat the alien invasion that poses a great threat, lomming over the horizon. Many enemy battle ships are about to test your skills in combat in open space. You are about to stand against them. Your objective is to shoot down as many enemies as possible and earn points. You start with a score of 0 and a moral level of 0.

Your star fleet is located at the bottom of the screen. It is represented by a single battle ship. You can move your battle ship around the game screen freely. Enemies are going to come your way. Your main goal is to shoot them with bullets. They are going to try and either hit you, resulting in certain and inevitable defeat straight away, or reach the bottom of the screen, thus making it to their target, on their way to earth and humanity. Each enemy that passes to the bottom of the scren reduces one of your life, which starts usually at 100. The moment it reaches 0, you also face defeat as well. However, there are ways to increase your life. Each level up gains you additional base life. Every level has a certain target you have to reach in order to win, a certain objective. Aside from this however, there is an endless mode, in which you face an endless enemy horde until you are defeated at last. That is your last stand where you can join the hall of fame - as every result in this game mode is then saved into the leaderboards.

Scoring

  • Each enemy you shoot down earns you a single point. missing with your shots results in a single point reduction.
  • If an enemy reaches the bottom of the screen without being shot down, you lose a life. This is your fort life.
  • If you reach a score of 500, your shoot delay will decrease to 750 milliseconds and your moral level will decrease by 5.
  • If your score is between -150 and -50, your shoot delay will decrease to 600 milliseconds and your moral level will decrease by 4.
  • If you lose all your lives, the game is over.
  • If an enemy hits you, the game is over.

Ship levels

There are five ship levels in total, each reached upon certain moral standpoint. You get various buffs upon each level up.
